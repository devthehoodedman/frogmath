Privacy Policy for FrogMath

Last Updated: May 14, 2026

At FrogMath, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information in our mobile application.

1. Personal Data Collection

FrogMath does not ask for, collect, or store any personal information that can identify the user (such as name, email address, or phone number).

2. Advertisements (Google AdMob)

We use Google AdMob to display advertisements to support the development of this game. To provide relevant ads, Google may use anonymous advertising identifiers linked to your device. These identifiers help limit the number of times you see an ad and may be used for personalized advertising.

3. Children's Privacy

FrogMath is an educational math game. We strive to ensure that all ads are filtered to be appropriate for all audiences, including children, in compliance with Google Play’s policies regarding family-friendly content.

4. Data Safety

We do not share any personal user data with third parties. Any data processed by Google AdMob is handled according to Google's own privacy standards.
